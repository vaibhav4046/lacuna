[span] 01 · CURRENT
[h3] Find what is true now.
[p] Lacuna resolves the standing claim and keeps the quote, source and observation time attached. The model receives an answer it can inspect, not a detached summary.
[button] Open the answer
[span] 02 · HISTORY
[h3] Keep the old fact without letting it win.
[p] Supersession is explicit. A correction changes the current answer while the earlier claim stays available for audits, timelines and handoffs.
[button] Inspect the timeline
[span] 03 · CONFLICT
[h3] Refuse to invent consensus.
[p] The billing-gate record names two runbook owners and neither source resolves the other. Lacuna preserves both claims and returns no answer.
[button] Inspect the conflict
[span] 04 · GRAPH + TABLE
[h3] Move through the field. Verify in rows.
[p] HydraDB holds the temporal evidence graph. The overview is for navigation; the proof path shows exact edges; the table makes every visible record readable.

[h3] Turn memory into governed work.
