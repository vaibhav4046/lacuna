What this measures, and what it does not
  The corpus is generated, and the graph is built from the same annotations
  the questions are scored against. A perfect score here says the structure
  survives the round trip: revision, retraction and disagreement are still
  distinguishable after ingestion, and the resolver reads them the way the
  corpus wrote them. It is a correctness check on the pipeline.
  It is not evidence that the approach beats anything, and it is not a
  measurement on real conversations. The comparison against recency,
  lexical, vector and hybrid retrieval runs on this same corpus through
  this same scorer, in scripts/benchmark.ts, and lands in artifacts/bench.
  That is where a claim of advantage has to be earned.
