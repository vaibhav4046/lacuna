## Video direction

The film inherits the accepted Lacuna system: `#000000` canvas, `#FFFFFF`
primary ink, `#9A9A9A` secondary ink, and `#FFB829` only for the exact mark's
origin dot and one proof highlight. Space Grotesk carries statements; JetBrains
Mono carries evidence labels. Authentic product capture may retain its own UI
accents, but the film frame never recolours the mark, adds purple framing, or
uses amber as a glow wash.

Motion uses smooth long-tail settles and a locked camera whenever the product
itself is moving. Real interaction is the hero. Editorial crops, rules and
labels reveal only when the narration names their proof; no frame dumps its
full hierarchy in the first quarter. Frames 5 and 10 end on deliberate held
reads; Frame 11 is the final quiet lockup. The lower 17% remains clear for
sentence captions. Never use bounce, lazy breathing, generic architecture
theater, partner-logo walls, fake cursors, simulated responses, or unlabelled
evaluation evidence. The forbidden failure modes are slideshow (front-load
then freeze) and screensaver (independent floating elements).
