LACUNA
Memory that knows what changed.

the source, time, standing, contradiction, retraction and absence behind every
answer, then compiles the bounded context the next agent needs.

The final V10 judge film must prove this with the accepted product: changed
facts with exact sources; unresolved contradictions; structural NO_EVIDENCE
abstention; interactive graph and readable table; one context across web, CLI
and MCP; HydraDB Cloud records, explicit relations and deterministic inspect;
governed no-write roles; truthful provider-unavailable voice fallback; and a
labelled generated 64-question evaluation that is not LongMemEval.

The exact Lacuna mark is a white open spiral with one #FFB829 amber origin dot
on black. Purple may exist inside authentic product capture, but never on the
mark or in the film's framing system.
